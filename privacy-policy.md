# Tiny Arena — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

Tiny Arena (Instagram: [@tinyarenasims](https://www.instagram.com/tinyarenasims/)) uses a small private app to manage **its own** Instagram account: publishing our own videos, reading our own post statistics, and reading and replying to comments left on our own posts.

## What data the app uses
- Content of our own Instagram account (our posts, captions, view and engagement statistics).
- Comments that people leave on our posts: the comment text, the commenter's public username, and the time it was posted. We use these only to read and reply to comments and to collect video ideas.

## What we do not do
- We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
- We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.
- We do not access any account other than @tinyarenasims.

## Storage
Comment text and usernames may be kept in a private working log so we know which comments we already answered. Nothing is published outside Instagram.

## Data deletion
To have any of your comments or data removed from our records, send a direct message to [@tinyarenasims](https://www.instagram.com/tinyarenasims/) on Instagram with the words "delete my data". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply. Deleting your comment on Instagram also removes it from our app's view.
