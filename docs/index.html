Tiny Arena

Tiny Arena makes short, original physics and math animations: pendulum waves, splitting balls, jelly, patterns and races. New videos are posted on Instagram (@tinyarenasims), Facebook and YouTube.

"Tiny Arena Tools" is a small private app the channel owner uses to manage these accounts: posting our own videos and reading our own video statistics. It has no outside users and no sign-up.

Contact: send a direct message to @tinyarenasims on Instagram.
