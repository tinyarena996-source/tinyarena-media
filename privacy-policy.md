# Tiny Arena — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-27

Tiny Arena (Instagram: [@tinyarenasims](https://www.instagram.com/tinyarenasims/), YouTube: Tiny Arena) uses a small private app, "Tiny Arena Tools", to manage **its own** accounts: publishing our own videos, reading our own post statistics, and reading and replying to comments left on our own posts. The app is used only by the channel owner. It has no outside users and no sign-up.

## YouTube API Services
Our app uses YouTube API Services to upload our own videos to our own YouTube channel and to read that channel's video statistics.
- By interacting with our YouTube channel through this app you agree to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).
- YouTube data is handled under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- The app only accesses the Tiny Arena channel that the owner authorized. It does not access any other YouTube user's account or private data.
- The owner can revoke the app's access at any time on the [Google security settings page](https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).

## What data the app uses
- Content of our own Instagram account and YouTube channel (our videos, titles, captions, view and engagement statistics).
- Comments that people leave on our posts: the comment text, the commenter's public username, and the time it was posted. We use these only to read and reply to comments and to collect video ideas.

## What we do not do
- We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
- We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.
- We do not access any account other than our own (@tinyarenasims on Instagram, Tiny Arena on YouTube).

## Storage
Comment text and usernames may be kept in a private working log so we know which comments we already answered. Video statistics are kept in a private log so we can see which videos work best. Statistics from YouTube API Services are refreshed or deleted within 30 days. Nothing is published outside Instagram and YouTube.

## Data deletion
To have any of your comments or data removed from our records, send a direct message to [@tinyarenasims](https://www.instagram.com/tinyarenasims/) on Instagram with the words "delete my data". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply. Deleting your comment on Instagram or YouTube also removes it from our app's view.
