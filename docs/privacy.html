Tiny Arena Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

Tiny Arena (Instagram: @tinyarenasims, YouTube: Tiny Arena) uses a small private app, "Tiny Arena Tools", to manage its own accounts: publishing our own videos, reading our own post statistics, and reading and replying to comments left on our own posts. The app is used only by the channel owner. It has no outside users and no sign-up.

YouTube API Services

Our app uses YouTube API Services to read the statistics of our own YouTube channel (for example views, watch time and audience retention of our videos), and may later be used to upload our own videos to that channel.

  • By interacting with our YouTube channel through this app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
  • YouTube data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • The app only accesses the Tiny Arena channel that the owner authorized. It does not access any other YouTube user's account or private data.
  • The owner can revoke the app's access at any time on the Google security settings page.

What data the app uses

  • Content of our own Instagram account, Facebook Page and YouTube channel (our videos, titles, captions, view and engagement statistics).
  • Comments that people leave on our posts: the comment text, the commenter's public username, and the time it was posted. We use these only to read and reply to comments and to collect video ideas.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.
  • We do not use the data for advertising or profiling.
  • We do not access any account other than our own.

Storage

Statistics about our own videos are kept in a private log so we can see which videos work best. Comment text and usernames may be kept in a private working log so we know which comments we already answered. If the owner revokes the app's access to YouTube, the YouTube data in these logs is deleted. Nothing is published outside our own social media accounts.

Data deletion

To have any of your comments or data removed from our records, send a direct message to @tinyarenasims on Instagram with the words "delete my data". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply. Deleting your comment on Instagram or YouTube also removes it from our app's view.

Contact

Direct message @tinyarenasims on Instagram.
